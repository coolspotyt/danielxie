A little about me

Maspeth, Queens.
Still where the story starts.

I’m Daniel. I grew up in Maspeth, Queens, and went to Xavier in Manhattan. I began at Purdue University in Indianapolis studying computer graphics, with a focus on animation and visual effects, before transferring to West Lafayette. I now study Communication, with a concentration in mass media and business communication and a minor in film and media.

I started working in hospitality as a teenager. Since then, business, film, marketing, and school have become parts of the same larger interest: how to build something and help people connect with it.

Outside work and class, I fly drones and make films. Sometimes that means telling a campus story; sometimes it means chasing the pace of New York with a camera.

Long term, I want to pursue business school and law school, and keep building through film, media, and marketing.

Have a project, an opportunity, or just something to say? I’d love to hear from you.
